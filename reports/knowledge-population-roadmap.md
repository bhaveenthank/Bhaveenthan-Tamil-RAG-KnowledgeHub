# Knowledge Population Roadmap

## Summary

This roadmap sequences future knowledge-population work after the extraction framework.
It does not authorize scraping or automatic extraction.

## Proposed Phases

| Phase | Goal | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Phase 30 | Annotated fixture design | Positive, negative, and ambiguous examples for each target. |
| Phase 31 | Entity and deity candidate extraction pilot | Reviewed entity/deity candidates with evidence spans. |
| Phase 32 | Author and work normalization | Accepted author/work relationships for verified corpora. |
| Phase 33 | Epithet pilot | Deity-epithet candidates with human review. |
| Phase 34 | Simile marker pilot | Marker-based simile candidates and false-positive analysis. |
| Phase 35 | Motif and theme pilot | Reviewed motif/theme candidates across a small sample. |
| Phase 36 | Relationship graph pilot | Links between accepted entities, motifs, devices, authors, works, and evidence. |

## Quality Gates

- Every accepted candidate must include a source record ID.
- Every accepted candidate must include a field name and evidence span.
- Every accepted candidate must include a source URL when available.
- Candidate promotion must be separate from candidate generation.
- Registry updates must be validated and benchmarked after promotion.

## Recommended Next Step

Start with annotated fixtures for entity, deity, and epithet examples. These targets are
foundational for later simile, metaphor, and relationship analytics.
